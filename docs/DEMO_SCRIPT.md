# Demo video script - Primer Peldaño Week 12 (3:30)

Record the live site at `https://primer-peldano-mx.vercel.app`. Rehearse from the full script, but record from the cue card at the end so the delivery sounds natural. Keep the cursor slow and do not enter real personal data.

## 0:00-0:20 - Open the door and name the problem

**On screen:** Open the live URL and pause on the title and prototype label.

**Say:**

“This is Primer Peldaño. It helps make useful work visible when that work happened outside a formal job title or credential. The goal is not to score a person. It is to connect evidence the worker already controls to the criteria for one real opening.”

## 0:20-0:55 - Start with the real stop-state

**On screen:** Point to `Uso real: sin compromiso`, the red stop-state, and the three employer actions.

**Say:**

“The first screen is intentionally blocked. Before asking a worker for time or evidence, an employer must verify one opening, publish its criteria, and commit to accepting specific evidence instead of rejecting someone because of an unfamiliar background. That commitment does not exist yet, so real use stops here. The software does not hide that gap.”

## 0:55-1:25 - Open the invented demonstration

**On screen:** Click **Abrir demo inventada**. Show the simulated label, invented employer, opening, and four criteria.

**Say:**

“I’ll open the invented demo. Everything here is simulated and labeled. The example is one operations opening with four declared criteria. This prevents the prototype from pretending that employer demand, a vacancy, or a hiring decision has already been verified.”

## 1:25-1:55 - Show worker-controlled evidence

**On screen:** Select `Pedidos de repostería`. Point to the privacy warning and the three editable fields. Make one small, harmless edit to the volume field, such as changing the number of orders.

**Say:**

“The worker begins with work that already happened, not a new unpaid test. They choose an example and control three short evidence fields: the situation, what they did, and the result. The privacy boundary appears before the inputs, and the demo rejects contact details, identifiers, credentials, and other sensitive-looking data.”

## 1:55-2:30 - Map evidence without scoring the person

**On screen:** Click **Mapear mi evidencia**. Point to `Sustentado`, `Parcial`, or `Sin evidencia`, and open one provenance disclosure.

**Say:**

“The mapping uses only the visible text. Each criterion is marked as supported, partial, or missing, with a reason and a visible source statement. Missing evidence stays missing. There is no global candidate score, ranking, verification claim, or prediction of who should be hired.”

## 2:30-3:00 - Preserve correction and human authority

**On screen:** Show the correction control and **Guardar copia local**. Do not let a download cover the screen.

**Say:**

“The worker can challenge the map, change the source text, and save a local copy. The public demo stores nothing. A human still has to inspect the evidence, and this prototype cannot verify truth, authorship, fairness, the existence of the opening, or an employment outcome. It demonstrates the interaction and its boundaries, not the venture result.”

## 3:00-3:30 - What changed my mind

**On screen:** Return to the top and leave the real stop-state visible.

**Say:**

“What changed my mind this week was realizing that the evidence map is not the first product decision. The employer commitment is. Without it, even a well-designed tool can turn into more unpaid work for the person it claims to help. That is why Primer Peldaño now opens blocked. The next decisive test is not another feature; it is one real employer accepting the evidence standard for one verified opening before a worker invests time.”

## Recording cue card

Use only these prompts while recording:

1. Useful informal work -> one opening -> no person score.
2. Real state blocked: opening, criteria, accepted evidence first.
3. Open invented demo; four criteria; everything simulated.
4. Worker edits existing evidence; privacy boundary.
5. Map visible text: supported / partial / missing + provenance.
6. Correct and export locally; human retains authority.
7. Mind changed: employer commitment is the first product decision.
