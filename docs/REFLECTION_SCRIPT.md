## Full rehearsal version

“The position that changed for me was what I thought the core product actually was. I entered the week believing the main value of Primer Peldaño was the evidence map: translate informal work into evidence connected to job criteria.

The build and the Blueprint changed that. If I ask a worker to prepare evidence before a real employer commits to considering it, I may create another unpaid task for the person I want to help. So I moved the employer commitment in front of the evidence map. The product now opens in a stop-state. Until an employer verifies one opening, publishes the criteria, and accepts named evidence types, the worker should not continue. That changed the sequence and authority of the product, not just its wording.

Against my plan, the venture is behind. The technical milestone is shipped: the public site works, the core flow is tested, and the demo states what it cannot prove. But the venture milestone is incomplete because the binding constraint is a real employer commitment. I hit it when I had to keep every record invented and block real use by default. Without that commitment, I cannot honestly test the workflow with a worker, claim a ten-to-one improvement, or show an employment outcome.

My next move is not another feature. It is to ask one employer with one verified opening to accept a specific evidence standard before any worker invests time. That outside commitment is the proof I need next.”
